## Methods

The Robert Koch Institute collects weekly casedata of a large variety of (vaccine-preventable) diseases per region in Germany. Accessible through https://survstat.rki.de/Default.aspx it is relatively easy to extract casenumbers of all of the diseases mentioned from the past 2 decades, give or take. Influenza in particular offers an interesting study groudn for studying the spatio-temporal spread of diseases in Germany.
